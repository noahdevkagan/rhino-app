## Product story
Headline: Why I built Rhino
Founder narrative:
I founded AppSumo, so I have spent years trying software that promises to save people time. I kept coming back to the same problem with dictation tools: many send sensitive conversations to the cloud or add more complexity than I want from a Mac app. I wanted to speak naturally and have clean text appear wherever I was working, without wondering who else could access my words.

I built Rhino as privacy-first dictation software for entrepreneurs, freelancers, creators, and anyone who thinks faster than they type. Speech recognition, optional AI cleanup, history, and personal dictionary data stay on the Mac. After the models are downloaded, Rhino can turn speech into text even when Wi-Fi is off.

I am keeping Rhino small and focused. My goal is to keep improving transcription quality and speed while protecting the local-first promise: talk, get useful text, and keep your voice on your own computer.

## FAQ
Q: Is this lifetime access?
A: Yes. One code includes lifetime access to Rhino and future product updates.

Q: How do I redeem my AppSumo code, and how many Macs can I use?
A: Follow the redemption instructions included with your AppSumo purchase. One code gives you access on unlimited Apple silicon Macs.

Q: Does my voice or transcript leave my Mac?
A: No. Audio, transcripts, history, and optional AI cleanup stay on your Mac; Rhino only connects for signed update checks and model downloads you start.

Q: Can Rhino work without an internet connection?
A: Yes. After you download your chosen speech model and optional cleanup model, dictation works offline.

Q: What are the system requirements?
A: Rhino requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are not currently supported.

Q: Which apps does Rhino work with?
A: Rhino inserts text into the Mac app where your cursor is active. It needs Microphone and Accessibility permissions, and modifier-only shortcuts such as Fn also require Input Monitoring.

Q: Can I control or delete my dictation history?
A: Yes. History stays on your Mac, and you can turn it off, delete individual recordings, clear everything, or set retention limits.
